=============================================================================
                            WHAT IT DOES
=============================================================================

1. Users search for schools by UDISE number
2. Page displays:
   - School name, UDISE, district
   - Contact information (coordinator, head master)
   - APPROVED Palak Melava parent-teacher meetings
   - APPROVED other school activities
   - Photos (clickable to zoom)
   - Video links

3. No login required (completely public)
4. Only APPROVED records shown (privacy)
5. Mobile responsive design
6. Modern UI with animations
