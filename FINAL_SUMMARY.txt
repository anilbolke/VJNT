🎯 WHAT THE PAGE DOES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Users can:

1. SEARCH SCHOOLS
   • Enter UDISE number in search box
   • Click Search button
   • Get instant results

2. VIEW SCHOOL DETAILS
   • School name
   • UDISE number
   • District name
   • Contact person information (coordinator, head master)
   • Clickable phone numbers
   • WhatsApp integration

3. VIEW PALAK MELAVA MEETINGS (APPROVED ONLY)
   • List of all approved parent-teacher meetings
   • Meeting dates
   • Chief guest information
   • Parent attendance numbers
   • 2 photos per meeting (clickable to zoom)
   • Full-screen photo viewer

4. VIEW SCHOOL ACTIVITIES (APPROVED ONLY)
   • List of all approved school activities
   • Activity dates and subjects
   • Guest information
   • Activity descriptions
   • 2 photos per activity (clickable to zoom)
   • YouTube video links

5. INTERACT WITH PAGE
   • Click photos to zoom (modal viewer)
   • Click video links to open YouTube
   • Click WhatsApp to start chat
   • Click phone to dial
   • Fully responsive on mobile
